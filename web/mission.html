04 / MISSION DEBRIEF · FDR-01

The prediction meets reality.

SIGNED

“Reality” here is a simplified model, not a flight-operations prediction. Exact Shapley shares use absolute contributions; signed contributions reconstruct the full-minus-ideal metric error. Cryogenic boil-off is integrated along the flown trajectory with sun, planet and eclipse geometry; the vented mass and cooler draw are signed and recomputed on verification. Vented mass is drawn from the same propellant pool the engine burns, so it reduces both the remaining fuel and the mass every acceleration divides by; running the pool dry by boil-off truncates thrust and is signed as a flameout. Tank volume, mixture split and ullage come from one derivation shared with the contract card, so a signed stage fits the airframe it was sized for. For frozen replay compatibility, v1–v3 certificates keep their original geometry and rules, schema 4 keeps catalog-dry-mass scoring, and current schema 5 scores fuel and prediction against verified fitted dry mass.
