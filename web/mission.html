02b / ASSEMBLE THE STAGE

Size it yourself, or delegate.

CHIEF ENGINEER

Each discipline can be sized by hand or handed to a rule-of-thumb chief. A delegated chief adds 35% mass and 30% cost to its own hardware, and caps the efficiency component of your score at 0.65. The margin is real dry mass, so it changes the flight. Your choice is signed into the recorder.

04 / MISSION DEBRIEF

The prediction meets reality.

“Reality” here is a simplified model, not a flight-operations prediction. Exact Shapley shares use absolute contributions; signed contributions reconstruct the full-minus-ideal metric error. Cryogenic boil-off is integrated along the flown trajectory with real sun, planet and eclipse geometry; the vented mass and cooler draw are signed into the recorder and recomputed on verification.
