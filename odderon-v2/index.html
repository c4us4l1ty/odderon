BURN DOCTRINE

Place a node at an apsis, align with SAS NODE, then arm and burn. The autopilot centres the burn on the node and trims on measured apsides.

WARP LIMITS

Warp is capped at 5× with the engine lit. Alignment always drops to 1×. Kepler propagation keeps the conic exact at any warp.

GRADING

Apsis accuracy is 62% of the score, propellant reserve 24%, time inside the window 14%. S ≥ 950, A ≥ 850, B ≥ 700, C ≥ 550.

The flight model is two-body with J2 and a thin exponential atmosphere below 200 km. It is simplified and labelled as such; it is not a certified trajectory tool.
